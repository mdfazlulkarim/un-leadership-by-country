Who leads the United Nations?

A country-by-country look at who has held the top leadership positions in the UN system, from the Secretary-General and the Security Council to the heads of the largest UN agencies, funds and programmes. Each part is built from public UN records and comes with open data you can download.

About the series

Every figure links back to its source, and the underlying data sits in plain CSV files in the project's GitHub repository, so it can be checked, reused and corrected. Each part adds to a shared country profile, so over time a single lookup shows a country's full record across the UN system.

UN Leadership by Country is an independent personal project. It is not an official United Nations publication and does not represent the views of the United Nations or any of its entities.
