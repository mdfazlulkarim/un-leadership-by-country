Peacekeeping has had a French head since 1997

Every head of UN peacekeeping since Bernard Miyet in 1997 has been a French national, including Jean-Pierre Lacroix, in post since 2017.

Other long-held posts

Since 2007, the political affairs department has been headed by United States nationals, the humanitarian office by British nationals, and the economic and social affairs department by Chinese nationals.

More women than men

Women head more of these departments and offices than men, reflecting the Secretary-General's 2017 system-wide strategy on gender parity.

Sources, method and data

Sources

  • United Nations, The United Nations System chart (March 2025), for the list of departments and offices.
  • United Nations Secretary-General, Secretariat leadership, and press releases on senior appointments. The source for every head is listed in the data file.
  • Map: world-atlas, derived from Natural Earth.

Method notes

  • The page covers the 30 departments and offices of the Secretariat in the UN System Chart. The Regional Commissions are shown on the UN agencies page, following the chart.
  • Monica Kathina Juma heads both UNODC and the UN Office at Vienna, so she appears twice and her nationality is counted twice.
  • The grouping by area of work is our own, to make the page easier to read. It is not an official UN classification.
  • Acting heads are counted as the current head and marked.
