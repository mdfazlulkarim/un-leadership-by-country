Bretton Woods tradition

Every President of the World Bank has been a United States national, and every Managing Director of the IMF has been European, under an informal arrangement dating from their founding.

UNICEF has always had an American head

All eight Executive Directors of UNICEF since 1946, from Maurice Pate to Catherine Russell, have been United States nationals.

WFP: American heads since 1992

Every Executive Director of the World Food Programme since Catherine Bertini in 1992 has been a United States national, including Luke Lindberg, appointed in 2026.

UNDP changed hands in 1999

UNDP was led by United States nationals until 1999. Since then it has been led by nationals of the United Kingdom, Türkiye, New Zealand, Germany and Belgium.

Recent firsts for women

Women now lead several agencies for the first time: the WTO (2021), ITU and IOM (2023), WMO (2024), and the OPCW and UN Tourism (2026).

Sources, method and data

Sources

  • United Nations, The United Nations System chart (March 2025), for the list and grouping of entities.
  • Each entity's official website and appointment announcements. The source for every head is listed in the data file and linked from each entity's pop-up.
  • Map: world-atlas, derived from Natural Earth.

Method notes

  • The page covers the entities outside the Secretariat shown in the UN System Chart. UNCDF and UNV, which are administered by UNDP, are not counted separately. Secretariat departments and offices have their own page.
  • For courts and tribunals (ICC, ITLOS), the President is shown. The ICC Prosecutor and Registrar head the Court's other organs.
  • Acting heads are counted as the current head and marked. The current head of UNICRI could not be confirmed from public sources.
  • Nationality is the one the UN and the entity use. "Since" is the year the person took up the post.
